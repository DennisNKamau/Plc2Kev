Frequently Asked Questions

Water testing helps determine whether your water is safe for drinking and other uses. It identifies contaminants such as bacteria, chemicals, heavy metals, and other impurities that may affect water quality and health.

It is recommended that water be tested at least once a year, especially for households, institutions, and businesses that rely on boreholes or private water sources.

Water vending machines are connected to a purification system that filters and treats water. Customers can then dispense clean drinking water using coins, tokens, or digital payment systems.

A water vending ATM machine is an automated system that dispenses purified drinking water to customers. These machines allow users to access safe drinking water conveniently while ensuring accurate and hygienic dispensing.
